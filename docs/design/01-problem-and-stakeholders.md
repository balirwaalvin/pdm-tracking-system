## 1.1 Service context

The **Parish Development Model (PDM)** is the Government of Uganda's strategy to move households from the subsistence economy into the money economy, using the parish as the lowest planning and delivery unit (MoFPED, PDM Implementation Guidelines, 2022 **[verify]**). Its financial-inclusion pillar is the **Parish Revolving Fund (PRF)**: each parish receives about **UGX 100 million a year** **[verify]**, managed by a parish **PDM SACCO**, which lends to households through **enterprise groups**. Loans are capped at **UGX 1 million per household** at **6% interest a year** **[verify]**. PDM targets households in the subsistence economy, estimated at about **39% of households** (UBOS, Uganda National Household Survey 2019/20 **[verify]**).

One loan passes through ten offices or roles, from the village to the ministry (section 1.3).

## 1.2 Problem statement

> Households applying for PDM loans cannot see where their application is, decisions and refusals are not recorded with reasons, and officials above the parish rely on paper summaries that arrive late. As a result, applications stall between offices, households are asked for unofficial payments to "push" files, complaints have no trail, and the ministry cannot see arrears or defaults until it is too late to act.

## 1.3 Stakeholders, needs and constraints

| Stakeholder | Role in the service | Needs | Constraints |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Household** (head of household) | Applies through a group, repays, raises complaints | Know their status, be treated fairly, a reason for any refusal, no unofficial charges | Often no smartphone or data; low literacy in English; travel cost to offices |
| **LC I Chairperson** | Confirms a household lives in the village | A quick way to confirm or reject, with a reason | Volunteer role; little time; may know applicants personally |
| **Enterprise group executive** | Prepares and submits members' applications | Know which members qualify; follow applications | Limited training; paper forms |
| **Parish Chief** | Profiles households; first office for complaints | One register; see what is pending | Many households; reporting burden |
| **Parish Development Committee (PDC)** | Oversees PDM in the parish | Visibility of progress and problems | Meets occasionally |
| **PDM SACCO officials** | Appraise, decide, pay out, collect repayments | Clear rules, a trail for every decision, repayment tracking | Small staff; must not decide their own appraisals (conflict of interest) |
| **Sub-county Chief / CDO** | Registers groups; supervises parishes; handles escalations | See problems across parishes; independent review of complaints and appeals | Many parishes |
| **District official** | Confirms fund releases reach SACCOs | Know releases arrived; district totals | Reports arrive late |
| **PDM Secretariat / MoFPED** | Releases the fund; national monitoring | National figures by district: paid out, repaid, at risk | Depends on reports from below |
| **Auditor** | Checks that rules were followed | A complete, tamper-evident record of who did what and when | Needs read-only access to everything |

## 1.4 The core problem in one line

**One loan, ten offices, no shared record.** The prototype gives every stakeholder the same live record, limited to their role and area.
